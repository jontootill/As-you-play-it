Dominion of Desire

All-Day Body Territory, Fortification & Conquest

How to Play

  • Win Condition: Hold majority control in all 6 body sections simultaneously.
  • Adjacency & Gate: Start on any open tile in an unlocked section, then expand to adjacent tiles. Pelvis & Intimate Zones unlocks once you hold majority in at least one qualifying section.
  • Natural Escalation: The whole board begins at Romantic, shifts to Sensual once 2 sections are controlled, and reaches Erotic at 4+ sections (or once Section 6 opens).
  • Takeovers & Fortifying: Contesting an opponent's tile lets you give the challenge for free or spend 1 Desire to receive it. On a turn, you may spend 1 Desire to Fortify an owned tile instead of claiming/contesting—raising its tier and doubling its duration.
